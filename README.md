# 3012 Jasper Blvd Structure Fire — Mount Pleasant After-Action Report

*Structure fire, 3012 Jasper Blvd, Sullivan's Island, SC | Saturday, February 22, 2025 | MPFD case (OCA) 25-0016238*

## Documents

  - [**After Action Report**](mpfd-after-action-report-3012-jasper.pdf) — Mount Pleasant Fire Department Form 201.07-01, 6 pages, issued by Captain Shane Himes, Engine Company 501. Dated 2/22/2025 (the incident date).
  - [**After Action Review (presentation)**](mpfd-after-action-review-slides-3012-jasper.pdf) — 36-page slide deck prepared by Captain Himes for the critique session (file dated 2-26-25). It includes the unit timeline, a minute-by-minute log of the first 80 minutes, and the discussion questions.

Both were obtained from the Town of Mount Pleasant through SC FOIA request TOMP-06769 (produced July 2, 2026).

The critique was attended by members of Mount Pleasant FD and Isle of Palms FD, including DC Harshaw (IOPFD), BC Chamberlin, BC Giddens (IOPFD) and others. No Sullivan's Island Fire Department members are listed as present.

## The incident in brief

  - **6:03 p.m.** — Wireless 911 call reporting a fire.
  - **6:08 p.m.** — Mount Pleasant Engine 501 sees smoke from the Ben Sawyer bridge and requests 3 more engines and a ladder.
  - **6:10 p.m.** — SIFD Engine 1201 and MPFD Engine 501 arrive within one second of each other. Engine 501 reports an elevated 2-story residence with smoke from the garage, establishes the water supply at Jasper and Station 30, and takes command ("E501 HAS CMD").
  - **6:13 p.m.** — SIFD Chief 1201 arrives and takes incident command.
  - **6:19 p.m.** — Fire knocked down on Division 1.
  - **6:36 p.m.** — Safety recommends pulling everyone out; **6:37 p.m.** evacuation tones are sounded.
  - **6:47 p.m.** — Structural collapse reported on the B/C corner, Division 3.
  - **7:11–7:12 p.m.** — Full roof collapse.
  - **9:21 p.m.** — Chief 1201 declares the fire under control.

Responding units: SIFD (Engine 1201, Chief 1201), Mount Pleasant (Engines 501, 502, 503, 505; Rescue 504; Ladder 503; BC508), Isle of Palms (Tower 1002, BC1006) and Charleston (Engine 116). Mount Pleasant's Battalion Chief 508 served as Safety.

## What the report found

### Staffing of the Sullivan's Island engine

Listed both as a gap in planning and as an unsafe behavior:

> *"E1201 was not adequately staffed as 2/4 of the firefighters were not qualified."*

### Command

> *"The initial establishment of command and later assumption of command was confusing and conflicting. This ultimately led to deployment/assistance with handlines by ladder companies and lack of completed primary and secondary searches."*

> *"C1201 arrived, and with disregard, took command without proper relinquishment of command. This led to confusion and conflicting fireground priorities."*

### Searches

> *"Primary searches and secondary searches were not completed on all divisions with survivable space. … During this period C1201 announced that the structure was clear without proper confirmation by completed primary searches."*

### Other issues

  - Re-establishment of the rapid intervention team (RIT) was not communicated, and no engine arrived to take RIT duties when it was requested.
  - The dispatch system recommended and sent the wrong units when Engine 501 asked for more help; requests must name specific units.
  - Calling the building an "elevated 2-story" house confused three companies, since it had three floors above ground.

### Recommendations

  - Add a second Mount Pleasant battalion chief to Sullivan's Island responses so the chiefs can run Operations, Accountability and Safety, "whereas SIFD automatically takes Incident Command."
  - Add Rescue 504 to the initial response, and send four engines not counting SIFD's.
  - The first-arriving battalion chief should take Operations and act as liaison to the SIFD chiefs.
  - Confirm primary and secondary searches, division by division, over the radio.

### What went well

Engine 501 knocked down the bulk of the first-floor fire and set up a water supply that later fed four handlines and a tower ladder. The safety and Alpha-side supervisor arrangement (BC508 and BC1006) worked as a liaison to SIFD command. Rescue 504's crews were described as "a crucial aid to fighting this fire."

## How the Town described it

At the March 3, 2025 Town Council session, Assistant Fire Chief Amanda Hawver told council: "We did our best to do an interior attack, but the roof started coming in, so we had to go exterior," and said "the only problem we did have… we didn't have very much water pressure." A WCSC Live 5 report, citing Town Administrator Joe Henderson, said all occupants and a dog got out and the cause was suspected to be electrical. Neither account mentions the staffing, command or search issues in Mount Pleasant's report.
